1. Overview

This website is operated by Crio Creatives, LLC (“Crio Creatives”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit criocreatives.com, contact us through the website, or interact with services embedded in or connected to the site.

We use information only for legitimate business, website-operation, security, analytics and advertising purposes, subject to applicable law.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

If you submit our project enquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, selected service and project message. If you contact us by email or another channel, we may also retain the information contained in that correspondence.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting, analytics and advertising providers may process technical information associated with requests to the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, page URL, referrer and approximate geographic information.

3. Contact forms

Our website contact form is processed using FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Form submissions are transmitted through FormSubmit so that they can be delivered to Crio Creatives by email.

We use enquiry information to review your request, respond to you, prepare estimates or proposals where appropriate, and maintain ordinary business correspondence. Submitting an enquiry does not create a contract or client relationship.

4. Website analytics

We use LibreCounter for lightweight website statistics. LibreCounter is loaded as an external image request and receives the page URL so that page views can be counted. Its published documentation states that it does not use browser cookies for analytics and does not store visitor IP addresses or user-agent strings as analytics records. It may derive aggregate information such as country, device type, browser and operating system from a request before storing aggregated statistics.

LibreCounter states that its server logs may temporarily contain IP addresses for operational and security diagnosis before deletion. LibreCounter statistics for a domain may also be publicly viewable.

5. Cookies & tracking technologies

Our current analytics tool, LibreCounter, does not use analytics cookies. However, other services used on this site — particularly advertising services — may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers.

Functional browser storage may also be used where needed to provide security, remember a temporary state, or support a requested website feature. We do not use that functional storage to build advertising profiles ourselves.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect how some third-party services or advertising features work.

6. Advertising & Google AdSense

Crio Creatives uses or may use Google AdSense and related Google advertising services. When Google advertising services are active, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Ad serving may also involve technologies such as web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers.

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings ↗︎. You can also learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites at Google’s partner-sites privacy page ↗︎, and about broader advertising choices at YourAdChoices ↗︎.

If additional third-party advertising vendors or networks are enabled in the future, this policy may be updated to identify or describe them as appropriate.

7. When information may be shared

We may share or allow processing of information by service providers that help operate the site and business, including website hosting, form delivery, analytics, email and advertising providers. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect our rights or systems, investigate abuse, or respond to lawful requests. We do not sell personal information collected through the project enquiry form.

8. Retention, security & your choices

We retain project enquiries and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary for business, record-keeping, legal or security purposes. Third-party services have their own retention practices.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have provided to us, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to retain certain information where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Because some providers operate internationally, information may be processed in countries other than the country from which you access this website.

9. Children

This website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly use the website to solicit personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, service providers, advertising setup or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
