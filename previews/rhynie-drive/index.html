EXPLORE THE PROJECT

Kitchen & Pantry.

01 / Interior renovation

Cream cabinetry, stone countertops and a warm timber island anchor the kitchen, alongside dedicated coffee and wine storage. The pantry combines open shelving, cereal dispensers, baskets and tall storage with timber base cabinets.

Living Room #1.

02 / Interior renovation

Cream seating and warm timber furnishings sit against a natural-stone accent wall, with doors opening to the terrace.

Powder Room.

03 / Interior renovation

A delicate botanical toile wallpaper brings warmth to the vessel-sink vanity and recessed toilet alcove, complemented by a round mirror, dark timber cabinetry, a display ledge and neutral floor tiles.

Living Room #2.

04 / Interior renovation

The upstairs lounge pairs cream seating and warm timber accents with palm-inspired wall art, a rattan-fronted media console and doors opening to an ocean-facing terrace.

Dining Area.

05 / Interior renovation

A dark timber dining table and upholstered chairs create a welcoming space beside large windows and terrace doors.

Master Bedroom.

06 / Interior renovation

A curtained timber canopy bed anchors the master bedroom, complemented by window-side seating and a warm timber media wall with TV storage.

Master Bathroom.

07 / Interior renovation

A new arched shower entrance, replacement lights above the vanity mirrors and one consistent floor tile treatment unify the bathroom, alongside the dual vanity and freestanding bath.

Bedroom 2.

08 / Interior renovation

Warm timber finishes and neutral textiles define the room, with a window-side seating nook, media wall, wardrobe and mirror.

Bedroom 3.

09 / Interior renovation

Two double beds sit against a layered tropical botanical feature wall, with natural textiles, a dressing table and a media wall.

Guestroom.

10 / Interior renovation

The downstairs guestroom combines a bold palm-silhouette feature wall, warm timber finishes, a dressing table and media wall with bright window-side seating.
