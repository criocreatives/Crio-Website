Renovation / Montego Bay, Jamaica

22B MaffeeDrive VillaRenovation

Kitchen and pantry, downstairs and upstairs living rooms, staircase landing, powder room, dining area and bedroom renovations.

EXPLORE THE PROJECT

Kitchen & Pantry.

01 / Interior renovation

The kitchen retains cream cabinetry, stone countertops, a timber island, and a dedicated coffee and wine station. Two pantry alternatives are shown: one with cream cubby shelving, and another with timber floating shelves, both incorporating tall storage and a chest freezer.

Living Room #1.

02 / Interior renovation

Cream seating and warm timber furnishings sit against a natural-stone accent wall, with doors opening to the terrace.

Powder Room.

03 / Interior renovation

Botanical toile wallpaper now defines the powder room, complementing the round-mirror vanity, white vessel sink, timber cabinet and the toilet alcove's half-height display ledge. The neutral tile floor remains consistent throughout.

Staircase Landing.

04 / Interior renovation

Two variations for the upstairs landing use warm timber slats, a tropical planter and woven accent lighting. One pairs botanical wallpaper with coastal artwork; the other uses a simpler wall with a bird-of-paradise print and timber wall sconce.

Living Room #2.

05 / Interior renovation

The upstairs lounge presents three complementary treatments around the vaulted exposed-rafter ceiling and ocean-facing terrace: a walnut-and-burgundy palette, a lighter coastal-neutral scheme with pale timber, and a tropical alternative with patterned accents. The adjoining dry bar features walnut-slat detailing, floating shelves, a coffee station and an undercounter beverage cooler.

Dining Area.

06 / Interior renovation

A dark timber dining table and upholstered chairs create a welcoming space beside large windows and terrace doors.

Master Bedroom.

07 / Interior renovation

A curtained timber canopy bed anchors the master bedroom, complemented by window-side seating and a warm timber media wall with TV storage.

Master Bathroom.

08 / Interior renovation

Botanical wallpaper adds texture to the vanity wall, alongside updated lights above the mirrors, a tiled arch framing the shower entrance, a freestanding bath and a consistent neutral floor tile treatment.

Bedroom 2.

09 / Interior renovation

Warm timber finishes and neutral textiles define Bedroom 2, with two double beds, a shared bedside table, a media wall, wardrobe and mirror.

Bedroom 3.

10 / Interior renovation

Two double beds sit against a layered tropical botanical feature wall, with natural textiles, a dressing table and a media wall.

Guestroom.

11 / Interior renovation

The downstairs guestroom combines a bold palm-silhouette feature wall, warm timber finishes, a dressing table and media wall with bright window-side seating.
