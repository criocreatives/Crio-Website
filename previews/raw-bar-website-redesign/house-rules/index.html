RawBar / Kingston

House rules.

Dress code

Dress for the occasion.

We favour a glamorous dress code. Dress with personality, purpose and originality.

  • No shorts, casual slides, flip-flops, beach shoes, Crocs or plastic/foam footwear.
  • No T-shirts or sportswear.
  • Gentlemen should wear collared or dress shirts.

Management reserves the right to refuse admission where the dress code is not met.

Reservations

Book ahead.

Reservations must be made at least one hour before the requested seating time.

Check official reservations for actual availability.

Service

Service charges.

RawBar's published menu states that a discretionary 12% service charge is automatically added to bills. Parties of eight or more attract an 18% service charge.

Visa, Mastercard and Discover are listed as accepted payment cards.

Dining

Food allergies.

Menu items may contain or come into contact with milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, nuts, wheat, soy and sesame. Consuming raw or undercooked food can carry additional risk.

Inform your server of any allergies before ordering. Read the official menu.
