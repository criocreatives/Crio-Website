VISUAL STUDY

Small footprint.
Complete perspective.

Tiny Living explores a compact residence through exterior perspectives, overhead layouts and interior views. The visual study follows the cabin from the surrounding landscape into its living spaces, kitchen, sleeping and work areas, and bathroom.
