PROJECT OVERVIEW

Quiet confidence. Considered detail.

At 9 Salisbury Avenue, Kingston, a residential interior shaped by natural light, layered neutrals and the warmth of timber. From the open-plan living area to private bedrooms and functional spaces, the design language remains purposeful and understated.

01 / LIVING

An atmosphere of ease.

Open views, tactile materials and a restrained palette establish a relaxed setting for everyday living.

DESIGN NOTE / 01 Timber accents and white brick introduce warmth and texture, while subtle lighting lets the space transition from daylight to evening.

02 / BEDROOM

Private spaces. Personal character.

A cooler accent palette complements the same warm joinery and soft finishes seen throughout the residence.

03 / THE DETAILS

Utility, beautifully resolved.

Storage, circulation and service areas receive the same attention to composition, lighting and finishes.

DESIGN NOTE / 02 Simple integrated cabinetry, floating elements and discreet illumination turn secondary spaces into a continuation of the architectural story.

04 / OUTDOOR LIVING

Room to step outside.

Glazed openings and an intimate balcony extend the rooms into a quiet outdoor retreat.
